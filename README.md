### AudioDAS – Distributed Audio Processing System

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/MamaniVicenteEver/audio-das-ia)

Distributed audio processing with .NET 9 and Next.js 16. The system orchestrates transcription (Whisper), summarization (Flan-T5), and filtering through isolated Python subprocesses, ensuring flexibility and zero external dependencies. Its concurrency engine uses in-memory channels with backpressure and 2-2-2 semaphores (jobs, FFmpeg, AI) to maintain 0% error rate under high load. It also performs client-side domain validation with ONNX Runtime and delivers instant streaming via HTTP 206.

## A Note on AI and Software Engineering

Artificial intelligence is transforming our profession as we know it. Many practices from just a few months ago are already becoming obsolete. However, continuously changing tools will not make you a better developer; it is your **foundational knowledge** that will. If you understand programming logic, design patterns, and architectural principles, AI can amplify your output—what used to take a month can now be accomplished in a week or even less. The syntax of any language may become ephemeral, but the logic behind it will always be your core asset. Invest in timeless skills: algorithms, data structures, and system design. They are the only true constants in our ever‑evolving field.
